# BookMySales.ai: Metrics

## 1. Overview

This document tracks BookMySales.ai's company-wide metrics, rolling up the individual client data in `customers-bms.md` alongside operational and market metrics referenced in the strategy document.

## 2. Core Strategic Metrics

| Metric | Current Value | Note |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Revenue Snapshot

- **Combined monthly recurring revenue (5 active Launch Special clients):** $14,300/mo
- **Average monthly fee per client:** $2,860/mo
- **Second-cohort pipeline:** currently in fit-assessment stage, not yet contributing revenue

## 5. Campaign Performance Snapshot

- **Average baseline meetings across cohort:** ~4.6/month per client, pre-engagement
- **Average guarantee target:** ~9.2/month per client (2x baseline)
- **Best-performing channel mix observed to date:** combined email + LinkedIn outperforming email-only outreach (per Northgate Financial Advisory campaign data)

## 6. How These Numbers Are Used

Consistent with the company's "outcomes over activity" value, these metrics are reviewed weekly by Client Success and monthly by Leadership, with particular attention to:

1. Is the guarantee actually achievable and repeatable across different industries? (fulfillment rate)
2. Are booked meetings translating into real pipeline, not just calendar volume? (meeting-to-opportunity conversion)
3. Does the model hold up once the guarantee period ends? (post-guarantee retention: the key open question for the launch cohort)
