# SharePoint Designs: references and case studies

Rule: include references only when the meeting notes ask for them or when they clearly
strengthen the case. Pick the closest match by product and industry. All of these are
published on the SharePoint Designs website.

- Kalsec (food ingredients, US): intranet redesign; about 40% less time to find content; 2x faster navigation to key tools.
- Renewal by Andersen: custom branded intranet; holiday intranet traffic up 80%.
- London Women's Clinic (healthcare, UK): template-based intranet across 13 clinics; intranet traffic up 80%.
- Ardena (life sciences, Belgium): custom LMS on SharePoint; third-party LMS licensing cost removed.
- Jefferson County Health Center (public health): SharePoint-native request cart; manual steps reduced from 7 to 1.
- Uno Minda (automotive): knowledge-management portal.
- Byou, Canada MedLaser, Rolling Suds: franchise knowledge and onboarding portals.
- Compass Group Denmark: bilingual knowledge base with approval workflows.
- Daikin: intranet connecting staff across 30+ countries.
- AVA Care: SOP management digitisation.
