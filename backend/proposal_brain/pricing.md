# SharePoint Designs: pricing, payment and support

## Pricing rules (apply in this order)
1. If the meeting notes state a price, discount or bundle (one-time setup, ongoing service, or setup plus ongoing), use it for that deal.
2. If not, use the default list prices and patterns in products.md and in this file.

- Invoice terms: payable 15 days from invoice date (see Terms, clause 10).
- Pricing structures used: fixed price per module; not-to-exceed price per phase; one-time licence; monthly managed support; hourly services.
- Discounts / bundles: [To be determined per client]

## Payment milestone patterns (defaults)
- Pattern A, module programme (e.g. intranet / Sangha): 25% on signature and kickoff; 25% when the foundation is live; 30% when the main modules are live; 20% on UAT sign-off and project completion.
- Pattern B, design-build-migrate programme (e.g. DocVault / document management): 25% on acceptance and kickoff; 35% on design approval and solution build review; 40% on migration, UAT and acceptance.

## Support plans (defaults)
- Intranet support tiers:
  - Essential Care, from $500/month: incident support, Microsoft platform-change monitoring, quarterly health check, governance and permissions guidance.
  - Continuous Improvement, from $1,000/month: everything in Essential Care, plus named success contact, monthly adoption reporting, content and navigation improvement, editor refreshers and agreed enhancement capacity.
  - Managed Employee Experience, from $3,000/month: everything above, plus proactive governance, department rollout support, adoption campaigns, content and UX optimisation, expanded enhancement capacity and quarterly sponsor roadmap review.
- DocVault / document management: 12-month Managed Support from $1,000/month: platform monitoring, Microsoft/SharePoint compatibility updates, up to 20 minor configuration or content changes per year, administration assistance, periodic optimisation reviews.
