# SharePoint Designs: standard text

## Terms and conditions (standard for every proposal; insert word for word)
1. Contract structure. This proposal, once signed by both parties, forms the statement of work and is governed by SharePoint Designs' standard services agreement. Where the two conflict, this proposal prevails on scope, price and schedule.
2. Change control. Any addition, removal or material alteration to scope must be agreed in writing through a change request stating revised price and schedule impact before work begins. Verbal approvals are not actionable.
3. Acceptance. Each deliverable is deemed accepted on the earlier of written sign-off or five business days after delivery to UAT without a logged defect. Defects logged during UAT are remedied at no cost; new requirements are change requests.
4. Post-launch care / warranty. Warranty support of [To be determined per client] from each module's go-live covers defects in delivered functionality. It excludes platform updates, client-side configuration changes, third-party API changes and new requirements.
5. Intellectual property. On receipt of full payment, the client owns all custom code, designs and configuration delivered for this engagement. SharePoint Designs retains ownership of pre-existing frameworks, libraries, accelerators and know-how and grants the client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use them within the delivered solution.
6. Client data and confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's confidential information in confidence and uses it only for this engagement. [Client] remains controller for personal data; SharePoint Designs acts as processor under a DPA and handles data only as instructed.
7. Data protection and security. Controls are maintained under the ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 programmes described above. [Client] is responsible for its tenant security posture, licensing, conditional access and retention policies.
8. Client responsibilities. Timely provision of environments, credentials, API access, named decision-makers, content and review turnaround within three business days at each gate. Client-attributable delays extend the schedule day-for-day and may attract standby charges after 10 cumulative business days.
9. Third-party dependencies. Third-party systems named in this proposal ([To be determined per client]) are outside SharePoint Designs' control. API, licensing or availability changes affecting scope are handled as change requests.
10. Payment and late payment. Invoices are payable 15 days from invoice date in USD. Overdue amounts may attract interest of 1.5% per month and work may be suspended after 30 days' non-payment on written notice. Prices exclude taxes, duties and withholdings.
11. Liability. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential loss, or loss of profit, revenue or data. SharePoint Designs' total aggregate liability is limited to total fees paid under this proposal, except where liability cannot legally be limited.
12. Non-solicitation and personnel. Neither party solicits the other's personnel engaged on this project for 12 months after project close without written consent. SharePoint Designs may substitute equivalent personnel and use vetted subcontractors under the same confidentiality obligations.
13. Termination. Either party may terminate for convenience on 15 days' written notice; the client pays for completed work and work in progress and SharePoint Designs hands over delivered work product and documentation. Material breach may be terminated after the agreed cure period.
14. Force majeure. Neither party is in breach for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control. Affected obligations are suspended for the duration.
15. Publicity and reference. SharePoint Designs may reference [Client]'s name and a general project description as a credential only after go-live. Screenshots, case studies and logo use require prior written consent.
16. Governing law and disputes. Governing law and jurisdiction are recorded in the executed services agreement. The parties first attempt good-faith sponsor escalation before pursuing other remedies.
17. Validity and entire agreement. This proposal is valid for 30 days from its date and, together with its assumptions and referenced services agreement, supersedes prior discussions, proposals and estimates.

## Change request procedure
- Request: changes or additions must be requested in writing by the client, with detailed specifications and the reason.
- Assessment: SharePoint Designs assesses the impact on cost, timeline and resources and submits an amended proposal.
- Acceptance: changes are implemented only on written acceptance of the amended proposal.
- Warranty: any impact on warranties is stated in the amended proposal.
- Charges: additional charges are detailed in the amended proposal and payable on its terms.

## Project management methodology
- Phased, milestone-based delivery with a named engagement lead accountable throughout.
- Weekly written status covering progress, risks, decisions pending and dependencies.
- Review and written acceptance at the close of each phase.
- Shared decision log and risk register from kickoff, with owners and dates.
- Fortnightly steering call with the client sponsor.
- Escalation to the SharePoint Designs engagement lead within one working day for any blocking item.

## Standard client responsibilities
- Microsoft 365 tenant access, licensing and named IT and business stakeholders by kickoff.
- A project sponsor with decision authority, and subject-matter experts for design validation and UAT.
- Decision-maker review within three business days at each gate.
- Tenant app-catalogue approval for SharePoint Designs components; identity / Entra group support.
- Routing of tenant-level changes through the client's change-management process.
- Content supplied in final form where content is the client's responsibility.

## Standard exclusions
- Microsoft 365, Copilot, Azure and other third-party licence or consumption costs.
- Content authoring, translation and migration of legacy content, unless scoped.
- Change-management or communications campaigns beyond any selected adoption package.
- Ongoing managed service beyond the warranty period, unless a support plan is selected.
- Archive or disposal of data the client elects not to migrate.
- Hardware and bespoke AI/automation work, unless separately scoped.

## Standard assumptions
- Integrations are limited to the systems named in the proposal.
- Later-phase estimates are reviewed with the client after earlier phases are validated.
- Final execution is governed by SharePoint Designs' standard services agreement / statement of work.
