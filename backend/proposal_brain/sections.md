# Master section list

Every section a SharePoint Designs proposal can contain, in document order.
- Core: suggested for every proposal. If the notes lack details, the section is still
  written from the brain, with [To be determined per client] for the gaps.
- Optional: suggested only when its "include when" condition is met by the meeting notes.
- Sources: notes = meeting notes, brain = company files, deal = per-deal values
  (usually [To be determined per client] unless the notes state them).

## A. Front matter
1. cover | Cover details | Core | Title, client name, client contact (prepared for), SharePoint Designs and signatory (prepared by), date, proposal number, validity. | notes + brain
2. key_facts | Business outcome and key facts | Optional | Include when the notes give a clear business outcome or headline facts (first release, duration, approach). | notes + deal

## B. Understanding the client
3. executive_summary | Executive summary | Core | The client's situation, what SPD recommends, and the recommended starting point. | notes + brain
4. what_we_heard | What we heard | Core | The client's priorities and requirements, as bullets in the client's own terms. | notes
5. revision_summary | Revision summary | Optional | Include only when the notes say this revises an earlier proposal; state what changed. | notes

## C. Solution and scope
6. solution_overview | Solution overview | Core | The SPD products/services proposed and how they fit together. | brain + notes
7. access_design | Access and permissions design | Optional | Include when the notes raise access, permissions, confidentiality or role-based visibility. | brain + notes
8. scope | Scope and deliverables | Core | Built automatically from the items the notes show as agreed for this engagement (see "scope decisions"); nothing else may be listed here. | notes
9. user_stories | User stories and permissions per scope | Optional | Include for detailed technical / SOW-style proposals where the notes describe user roles and their needs. | notes + brain
10. optional_addons | Optional add-ons | Optional | Include when the notes mention products the client may add later or wants priced separately. | brain + notes
11. phases | Phases and roadmap | Optional | Include when the notes discuss a phased rollout, later phases or future workstreams. | notes + brain
12. interactive_preview | Interactive preview | Optional | Include when the notes mention a demo, preview or showing decision-makers before signing (mainly Sangha / intranet). | brain
13. technical_approach | Technical architecture / approach | Optional | Include when the notes raise IT, architecture, tenancy, integration or AI-platform questions. Write only the specifics the notes/brain actually state — do not add technical mechanism detail (e.g. frameworks, permission models) that wasn't said. | brain + notes

## D. Delivery
14. timeline | Delivery approach and timeline | Core | Stages from the brain's delivery patterns; durations from the notes, otherwise [To be determined per client]. | brain + notes + deal
15. methodology | Project management methodology | Optional | Include for larger or multi-phase programmes, or when governance/reporting is discussed. | brain
16. change_control | Change control | Optional | Include for larger or multi-phase programmes; otherwise it is covered by Terms clause 2. | brain
17. project_team | Project team | Core | Table: Name, Role, Responsibility. Fill rows only with people named in the notes; otherwise leave the rows empty for the reviewer to fill. | notes
18. client_responsibilities | What we need from the client | Core | Standard client responsibilities plus anything specific from the notes. | brain + notes
19. training_handover | Training, launch and handover | Optional | Include when the notes mention training, rollout, adoption or handover. | brain + notes

## E. Commercials
20. investment | Investment | Core | Built automatically: one price line per agreed item, priced from the notes first, then brain defaults marked (to be confirmed), otherwise [To be determined per client]. | notes + brain + deal
21. payment_schedule | Payment schedule | Core | Milestone pattern A or B from pricing.md, matched to the programme type, with each milestone's percentage marked (to be confirmed) since it's a brain default. Rename any milestone whose brain label doesn't match the notes (e.g. drop "migration" from the name/description if the client is enhancing existing content, not relocating it) — the brain's wording is a starting point, not a quote to copy. | brain + deal
22. exclusions | Exclusions | Core | Standard exclusions plus anything specific from the notes. | brain + notes
23. assumptions | Assumptions and dependencies | Core | Standard assumptions plus client-specific ones from the notes. | brain + notes
24. warranty | Warranty / post-launch care | Core | Warranty coverage from pricing.md; period [To be determined per client] unless the notes state it. | brain + deal
25. support_plans | Optional managed support plans | Optional | Include when the notes mention ongoing support, maintenance or a monthly fee. | brain + notes

## F. Trust and close
26. why_spd | Why SharePoint Designs | Optional | Include for new clients or competitive evaluations. Pick the points that fit the client's industry. | brain
27. security_compliance | Security, compliance and insurance | Optional | Include when the notes mention security, compliance, certifications, GDPR, audits or data handling. If the notes show the client asking for supporting materials (architecture docs, deployment docs, security/compliance certifications, client references) to accompany the proposal, list each one explicitly as a line marked [To be determined per client] — introduce the list as still to be provided, never as already attached or included, since every line is a placeholder. | brain + notes
28. terms | Terms and conditions | Core | Insert the standard terms from standard_text.md word for word, filling only [Client] and the bracketed placeholders. | brain
29. open_items | Open items to be confirmed | Core | Built automatically from the items the notes show as discussed, suggested or awaiting a client decision, but not agreed; each marked (to be confirmed). | notes
30. spd_next_steps | Next steps from SharePoint Designs | Core | Built automatically from the follow-up tasks SharePoint Designs said it would do itself; each marked (to be confirmed). | notes
31. signatures | Acceptance and signatures | Core | Client signatory (from the notes, else the client contact) and SharePoint Designs signatory. | notes + brain
